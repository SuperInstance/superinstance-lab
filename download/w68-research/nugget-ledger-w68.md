## Field A — Bacterial quorum sensing + plasmid-mediated horizontal gene transfer
**Why chosen:** cell-fleet's learning is purely VERTICAL (parent→child mutation at mitosis). Bacteria are the one mature field where populations (a) gate collective action on a threshold statistic of the population and (b) learn LATERALLY by copying sub-genomes across unrelated cells. Both are missing organs in the fleet.

**Nuggets:**
- **A1 · Autoinducer threshold = density-gated collective commitment.** Cells secrete autoinducer and switch behavior (biofilm, virulence, competence) only when the EXTRACELLULAR concentration crosses a threshold — the cell acts on a statistic of the population, not its own state, so no individual pays the cost of collective action until the group is likely to succeed. Sources: "Methods for Analysis of Bacterial Autoinducer-2 Production" (ResearchGate, Jul 2026); "The Collective Power of Bacteria as a Blueprint" (Bristol, Sep 2026); AI-2 QS corpus (PMC). **Mapping:** cell-fleet's mitosis/apoptosis alarms read only LOCAL state. The KV membrane ALREADY carries the autoinducer — every `cell:{id}` row publishes affinity. A quorum organ = Tissue DO counts receipt-verified rows above threshold before committing tissue-level acts (mitosis at CAP, organ-boot ticket, reseed). JEV/exoj analogy: quorum is an explicit observation event on an aggregate.
- **A2 · Signal mimicry — the channel is spoofable, so claims need provenance.** A human host metabolite MIMICS bacterial AI-2 and activates QS (Ismail et al., Princeton OAR / ScienceDaily "Discovery of a Human Metabolite That Mimics the Bacterial QS Autoinducer AI-2"); "Targeting quorum sensing for manipulation of commensal communities" (Mendeley review) — receivers cannot distinguish kin-signal from mimic. **Mapping:** any affinity/priority number published in a shared membrane can be forged by a mimic cell (directly feeds C4 below). Fleet law candidate: membrane claims must reference a hash-chained ledger tip (cell-fleet already ships `genomeHash` in rows — extend to affinity receipts), or judge-relay samples and audits claims.
- **A3 · Conjugation = lateral learning with a transmission/carriage trade-off.** Plasmids evolve on a trade-off: high transfer rate favored when horizontal opportunities abound, low metabolic burden favored when carriage selection dominates ("Increased copy number couples the evolution of plasmid transfer rate", St Andrews; "The metabolic burden associated with plasmid acquisition", Semantic Scholar); conjugation itself causes dynamic, regulatable transcriptional changes in the recipient (bioRxiv, "Bacterial species and surface structures shape gene flow during conjugation"); donors gain fitness from giving ("Plasmid transfer confers a fitness advantage to donor cells"). **Mapping:** THE missing learning channel. A "plasmid" = one morphogen channel's weight slice + bias, published to KV as `plasmid:{channel}:{hash}`; any cell may conjugate on task contact and pays a learning-rate (η) penalty while carrying foreign weights; the DONOR gets a receipt credit (fitness advantage to donor). Spreads a good decomposition trick across unrelated lineages without common ancestry.
- **A4 · Private channels / cross-talk shape coalitions.** Diverse QS signal/receptor pairs create private channels; cross-talk determines who coordinates with whom (bioRxiv, "Diverse quorum sensing systems regulate microbial"; Frontiers reviews). **Mapping:** organ-dialect receipt law is already a private channel; a tissue can run several signal channels (per morphogen vocab) and gate different collective actions per channel — organ-boot-loader tickets as a dedicated channel isolated from the decomposition channel.

## Field B — Viral quasispecies: error thresholds, survival of the flattest, lethal mutagenesis
**Why chosen:** the fleet EVOLVES by mutation (MUT_SIGMA=0.08 per mitosis) and nobody has ever asked whether that mutation budget is safe. Quasispecies theory is the quantitative science of exactly this question, and it is computable against the receipted run.

**Nuggets:**
- **B1 · Error threshold (Eigen):** selection maintains a master sequence only while mutation × genome length is small enough; beyond the critical rate the population clouds and inherited information melts (Eigen's paradox framing: PhilArchive "The Semiotic Barrier of Being"; Manrubia, "Towards the Next Generation of Quasispecies Models", PubMed). **Mapping:** fleet genome = w[8]+b (9 effective reals, tiny information content → error threshold FAR from operating point for extinction); but the sharper fleet-relevant threshold is IDENTITY retention (does the best cell's fitness state survive mitosis?), measured this wave (see simulation).
- **B2 · Survival of the flattest:** at high mutation rates, selection favors robust plateaus over narrow peaks — expected lineage fitness = peak height discounted by neighborhood robustness (Wilke et al. 2001 canon; fresh: "Evolution on the biophysical fitness landscape of an RNA virus", UTMB; "Epistasis and the changing fitness landscapes of SARS-CoV-2", Sep 2026; "Mutational robustness of an RNA virus influences sensitivity to mutagenic drugs", PubMed). **Mapping:** MITOSIS_ABOVE gates on peak height (EMA) only. Fleet robustness = children of a genome still decompose well; cheap proxy: mutate the parent k times, score with the rubric offline, gate mitosis on robustness×EMA.
- **B3 · Lethal mutagenesis:** raise the error rate and the population collapses — a working antiviral strategy (fresh: clitocine vs SARS-CoV-2, RSC Sep 2026; "Prion-based protein self-assembly tunes mutagenesis", Cell Aug 2026; "Optimization of DNA polymerase mutation rates", PNAS). **Mapping:** DEFENSIVE law — a poisoned plasmid (A3) or corrupted mitosis seed is lethal mutagenesis for the fleet; conversely a mutational-load detector (genomeHash distance from founder per lineage, in the tissue report) can force apoptosis before the ledger fills with junk — the receipted senescent founder (EMA 0.5916) was exactly a lineage past its own threshold.
- **B4 · Bottlenecks make drift dominant.** Repeated single-cell bottlenecks purge and fix variants stochastically; transmission bottlenecks shape quasispecies composition ("Genetic Diversity Found after Single-Cell Bottlenecks"). **Mapping:** cell-fleet mitosis IS a single-cell bottleneck; with the real landscape flat (live EMA std 0.0043) and mutation noise (real sibling delta std 0.0097) 2.3× WIDER than the landscape contrast, drift beats selection among survivors — MIN_TASKS / routing concentration is what makes selection bite, not mutation tuning.

## Field C — Bitcoin mempool fee markets: mechanism design under congestion
**Why chosen:** the receipted wave-67 ecology died of STARVATION (12 of 13 apoptoses) under least-recently-served routing + trickle load. The mempool is the mature engineering field for allocating a scarce capacity channel among competing claims under congestion — with 15 years of adversarial hardening.

**Nuggets:**
- **C1 · Fixed capacity ceiling ⇒ admission gate = fee floor.** Bitcoin encodes hard per-block weight limits; under congestion the mempool becomes a priority queue ordered by fee rate ("Structural Tensions in the Bitcoin Protocol", ResearchGate Sep 2026; "Transaction fee market design for parallel execution", arXiv 2502.11964; "Transaction Fee Mechanisms under Execution Uncertainty", arXiv Jun 2026; "The Execution Dilemma in Pessimistic Blockchains", Dagstuhl Oct 2026). **Mapping:** the atlas's 67 ADMISSION gates are fee floors — minimum evidence required to enter a work; and tissue routing should be fee-priority (expected rubric reward per task cost) instead of least-recently-served when the task stream is congested. The starvation regime is exactly a mempool backlog problem.
- **C2 · Replace-by-fee (RBF): stuck work re-bids instead of rotting.** A stuck unconfirmed transaction raises its fee to jump the queue (full-RBF; "What Really Happens to Unconfirmed Bitcoin Transactions?", d-central Jul 2026; paywithflash fee calculator docs). **Mapping:** a cell holding a task it cannot decompose well re-bids: ribosome re-runs `translate()` with a mutated genome (cheap explore move) to raise expected reward before giving up — "stuck tasks get re-processed, not just re-queued". JEV mapping: re-bids are soft deformations; block inclusion = observation.
- **C3 · Child-pays-for-parent (CPFP) / package relay: descendants sponsor ancestors.** Bitcoin Core v28 one-parent-one-child package relay lets a low-fee parent confirm because a high-fee child is economically attached to it (cryptoslate Nov 2025; chemaclass "How Bitcoin Works" Dec 2025). **Mapping:** reward attribution: today the reward accrues ONLY to the executing cell. CPFP analog: a valuable decomposition PART credits its ancestor chain (upstream lineage EMA α-discount) — organ-boot-bridge: the organ that eventually boots sponsors its boot ticket.
- **C4 · Ordering is gameable: selfish ordering, replacement cycling.** Statistical audit shows mining pools deviate from ordering norms to accelerate their own transactions (ACM, "Selfish & opaque transaction ordering in the Bitcoin blockchain"); replacement-cycling attacks exploit RBF+CPFP economics with junk children (ariard.github.io). **Mapping:** honest-negative design law: ANY priority market in tissue routing creates selfish-mining for cells — a cell can inflate its membrane affinity to attract routing. Mitigation = A2: priority claims must be receipt-backed and judge-relay-audited; otherwise fee-priority routing selects for liars.
