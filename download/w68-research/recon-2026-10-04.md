## 2. Deep-read 1 — exoj `gan/`: Bridge 3, the Unit Table GAN (+ sxc1 dialect)

Files read: gan/{constants,unitTable,generator,validator,scars,envelope,die}.mjs,
README.md (wave-69 section), lab/gan.test.mjs (run: 26/26 pass),
experiments/outputs/w69_bridge3_demo.json (re-generated byte-identical, exit 0).

What it is: a two-cell adversarial pair on a dynamic vector table. Cell 01
(generator, profile "high-prefill, low-latency") nudges the lightest row and a
hash-chosen subset of neighbours toward a scaled copy of the densest row's
direction inside a pre-registered amplitude budget, then rescales masses —
conserving total mass exactly (densest touched row absorbs the residual). Cell 02
(validator, profile "specification-first") re-derives every metric and answers
ONLY `COMPILED | INDETERMINATE` with named fail-closed codes; refused tables
never enter the pipeline and leave one sticky scar per code. Constants are
pre-registered with inline provenance from quilt-murmur's e40 telemetry (a
dead detector's measured floor becomes a living boundary). All surprise comes
from a deterministic d20 die (sha256(seed:d20:nonce)), whose 20 named structural
drifts may cross the proximity boundary but are then scarred, not refused.
Cross-language state exchange uses `sxc1` envelopes with a float-free value
grammar so JS and Python derive identical hashes.
