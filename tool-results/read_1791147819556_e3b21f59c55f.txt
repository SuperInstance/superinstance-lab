     9→
    10→**Category-theoretic investigation of the inverted field**
    11→
    12→### 1. The ambient category
    13→
    14→Let **Field** be the category whose  
    15→
    16→- **objects** are relational fields \(F = (C, \{\gamma_c,\eta_c,\Delta_c,I_c\}_{c\in C})\)  
    17→  (cells on a lattice together with the three amplitudes and identity-fragment sets),  
    18→
    19→- **morphisms** \(F\to F'\) are lattice-respecting maps of cells that  
    20→  – weakly preserve the amplitudes (soft, convex combination),  
    21→  – send identity fragments only by inclusion (never by forced identification),  
    22→  – commute with the global conservation inequality  
    23→    \(\overline{\gamma}+\overline{\eta}\le 1\).
    24→
    25→Composition is ordinary composition of the underlying cell maps; identities are the identity maps on cells.  
    26→This is precisely the category **CSPersist** of the Hermit-Crab paper, re-indexed from “constrained systems” to “fields”.
    27→
    28→### 2. Observers as functors
    29→
    30→An observer \(o\) is a functor  
    31→
    32→\[
    33→O_o : \mathbf{Field} \to \mathbf{Set}
    34→\]
    35→
    36→that sends a field to the set of cells it currently attends to, and a deformation to the induced restriction.  
    37→Parallel observation is simply a family of such functors \(\{O_o\}_{o\in O}\).
    38→
    39→### 3. Deformation as a natural transformation
    40→
    41→A deformation \(d\) is a natural transformation  
    42→
    43→\[
    44→d : \mathrm{Id}_{\mathbf{Field}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{Id}_{\mathbf{Field}}
    45→\]
    46→
    47→whose component at each cell is the soft convex update  
    48→
    49→\[
    50→(\gamma,\eta,\Delta)\;\mapsto\;(1-\alpha)(\gamma,\eta,\Delta)+\alpha(\gamma',\eta',\Delta').
    51→\]
    52→
    53→Naturality says that the update is independent of the order in which observers attend; that is exactly the parallel-first axiom.
    54→
    55→### 4. The projection (the old “tape”) is a right Kan extension
    56→
    57→Fix an observer \(o\).  
    58→The first-person causal sequence (the old mark-tape) is the **right Kan extension**
    59→
    60→\[
    61→\mathrm{Ran}_{O_o}(\mathrm{Id})\,:\,\mathbf{1}\to\mathbf{Field}.
    62→\]
    63→
    64→Explicitly, it is the limit, over the comma category of cells attended by \(o\), of the field restricted to those cells, linearised by the temporal order of deformations.  
    65→
    66→Because a right Kan extension is universal, any other sequential presentation of the same attended region factors uniquely through this one.  
    67→Hence every first-person log is a derived object; the field is primary.
    68→
    69→(This is the same universal property that the Hermit-Crab paper proves for the nesting topology: the hermit-crab pattern is the left Kan extension that solves the agent-embedding problem.  Here the dual statement appears for sequentialisation.)
    70→
    71→### 5. Conservation and creative band as limits
    72→
    73→- The conservation axiom is the statement that the terminal object of **Field** (the “fully crystallised” field) is a limit cone for the diagram of all finite attended sub-fields.  
    74→- The creative-band preference is a weighted colimit: deformations whose \(\Delta'\) lies in \([0.4,0.6]\) are the generators that receive maximal weight in the colimit that produces the next field state.
    75→
    76→### 6. Identity fragments as a discrete fibration
    77→
    78→The assignment \(c\mapsto I_c\) is a discrete fibration  
    79→
    80→\[
    81→I : \mathbf{Field}\to\mathbf{Set}.
    82→\]
    83→
    84→The “never collapse” rule is exactly the statement that this fibration has no non-trivial descent data; i.e. it is a stack for the trivial topology.  
    85→Molting (change of shell) is a cartesian lift in this fibration—the same cartesian-lift language used for identity preservation in the Hermit-Crab paper.
    86→
    87→### 7. Adjunction that recovers the old sequential tools
    88→
    89→There is an adjunction  
    90→
    91→\[
    92→\mathrm{Project}\;\dashv\;\mathrm{Embed}
    93→\]
    94→
    95→where  
    96→
    97→- \(\mathrm{Project}\) sends a field + observer to its right-Kan-extension sequence,  
    98→- \(\mathrm{Embed}\) sends a sequence back into the free field generated by that sequence.  
    99→
   100→The unit of the adjunction is “take the sequential log you already have and regard it as a field”; the counit is “project the field you already have down to one observer’s log”.  
   101→All of the earlier `jmark` / tape machinery is the concrete computation of this adjunction.
   102→
   103→### 8. Summary in one line
   104→
   105→\[
   106→\text{Field}\;=\;\text{primary object of }\mathbf{Field},
   107→\qquad
   108→\text{Process}\;=\;\mathrm{Ran}_{O}(\mathrm{Id}),
   109→\qquad
   110→\text{Conservation + creative band + identity}\;=\;\text{limits / colimits / discrete fibration}.
   111→\]
   112→
   113→Everything sequential is a Kan extension; everything parallel is native.  
   114→That is the precise categorical content of the inversion.

   118→**Killer-app: ExoJ — external vectorized scratch-paper.**
   119→
   120→An agent works *outside* its own forward pass on a live quilt projection of a parallel relational field. JEV emits soft logical vectorized progressions into that surface. The wave-function stays open (chain-of-probabilities). Observation is an explicit, recorded, local act — not the default. The agent can “show its work” as a dependent projection without ever having collapsed the intermediate possibilities.

   122→### Architecture (built)
   123→
   124→1. **Field** — primary object (parallel relational amplitudes on a hex lattice).  
   125→2. **Quilt** — spatial projection = the visual spreadsheet the agent actually sees and writes on.  
   126→3. **JEV** — inference engine that only ever emits soft deformations (classical / JEPA-style / quantum-inspired / cellular-LLM backends).  
   127→4. **Observer interface** — agent attends, deforms, optionally observes, can request a first-person projection.  
   128→5. **Audit** — every deformation is content-addressed; the field itself is the objective proof object.

   242→### 2. Core runtime (`exoj_core.py`)
   243→
   244→```python
   245→#!/usr/bin/env python3
   246→"""
   247→ExoJ core — vectorized scratch-paper
   248→Field is primary. Quilt is the projection. JEV emits soft vectorized progressions.
   249→No forced collapse. Observation is an explicit, recorded act.
   250→"""
   251→
   252→from __future__ import annotations
   253→import json, hashlib, time, math, random
   254→from dataclasses import dataclass, field
   255→from typing import Dict, List, Tuple, Optional, Any
   256→from pathlib import Path
   257→
   258→def hex_dist(q1, r1, q2, r2):
   259→    return (abs(q1-q2) + abs(q1+r1-q2-r2) + abs(r1-r2)) // 2
   260→
   261→@dataclass
   262→class Cell:
   263→    q: int
   264→    r: int
   265→    gamma: float = 0.0
   266→    eta: float = 1.0
   267→    delta: float = 0.5
   268→    strength: float = 0.0
   269→    frags: List[str] = field(default_factory=list)
   270→    programs: List[str] = field(default_factory=list)
   271→    touched: int = 0
   272→    prob_mass: float = 1.0
   273→
   274→    def soft_write(self, g, e, d, alpha=0.4):
   275→        self.gamma = (1-alpha)*self.gamma + alpha*g
   276→        self.eta   = (1-alpha)*self.eta   + alpha*e
   277→        self.delta = (1-alpha)*self.delta + alpha*d
   278→        self.strength = max(self.strength, g)
   279→        self.prob_mass = min(1.0, self.prob_mass * 0.98 + 0.02)
   280→
   281→    def to_dict(self):
   282→        return {
   283→            "q": self.q, "r": self.r,
   284→            "γ": round(self.gamma,4), "η": round(self.eta,4), "Δ": round(self.delta,4),
   285→            "str": round(self.strength,4), "prob": round(self.prob_mass,4),
   286→            "frags": self.frags[-4:], "programs": self.programs[-3:],
   287→            "touched": self.touched,
   288→        }
   289→
   290→class ExoJ:
   291→    def __init__(self, name: str = "exoj", radius: int = 4):
   292→        self.name = name
   293→        self.radius = radius
   294→        self.cells: Dict[Tuple[int,int], Cell] = {}
   295→        for q in range(-radius, radius+1):
   296→            for r in range(-radius, radius+1):
   297→                if hex_dist(q,r,0,0) <= radius:
   298→                    self.cells[(q,r)] = Cell(q,r)
   299→        self.seq = 0
   300→        self.deformations: List[Dict] = []
   301→        self.observations: List[Dict] = []
   302→        self.observers: Dict[str, Dict] = {}
   303→
   304→    def _norm(self):
   305→        active = [c for c in self.cells.values() if c.touched > 0]
   306→        if not active: return
   307→        g = sum(c.gamma for c in active)/len(active)
   308→        e = sum(c.eta for c in active)/len(active)
   309→        s = g+e
   310→        if s > 1.001:
   311→            for c in active:
   312→                c.gamma /= s
   313→                c.eta   /= s
   314→
   315→    def attend(self, observer: str):
   316→        self.observers[observer] = {"seq": self.seq, "t": time.time()}
   317→        return list(self.observers.keys())
   318→
   319→    def jev_emit(self, q: int, r: int, gamma: float, eta: float, delta: float,
   320→                 tag: str = "", program: str = None, backend: str = "classical"):
   321→        """JEV step: soft vectorized progression. Never forces a definite token."""
   322→        key = (q,r) if (q,r) in self.cells else (0,0)
   323→        cell = self.cells[key]
   324→        cell.soft_write(gamma, eta, delta)
   325→        cell.touched = self.seq + 1
   326→        frag = hashlib.sha256(f"{self.name}|{q}|{r}|{self.seq}|{backend}".encode()).hexdigest()[:8]
   327→        if frag not in cell.frags:
   328→            cell.frags.append(frag)
   329→        if program:
   330→            cell.programs.append(program)
   331→        self.seq += 1
   332→        ev = {
   333→            "seq": self.seq, "q": q, "r": r,
   334→            "γ": round(gamma,4), "η": round(eta,4), "Δ": round(delta,4),
   335→            "tag": tag, "backend": backend, "program": program,
   336→            "t": time.time(),
   337→        }
   338→        self.deformations.append(ev)
   339→        self._norm()
   340→        return ev
   341→
   342→    def observe(self, q: int, r: int, definite: float):
   343→        """Explicit observation = only act that collapses local prob mass."""
   344→        key = (q,r) if (q,r) in self.cells else (0,0)
   345→        cell = self.cells[key]
   346→        old_prob = cell.prob_mass
   347→        cell.prob_mass = 0.0
   348→        cell.delta = definite
   349→        self.seq += 1
   350→        obs = {
   351→            "seq": self.seq, "q": q, "r": r,
   352→            "definite_Δ": definite, "prev_prob": round(old_prob,4),
   353→            "t": time.time(),
   354→        }
   355→        self.observations.append(obs)
   356→        return obs
   357→
   358→    def sense(self) -> Dict:
   359→        active = [c for c in self.cells.values() if c.touched > 0]
   360→        if not active:
   361→            return {"γ":0,"η":1,"Δ":0.5,"Σ":1,"active":0,"zone":0,"prob_open":1.0,"observers":[]}
   362→        g = sum(c.gamma for c in active)/len(active)
   363→        e = sum(c.eta for c in active)/len(active)
   364→        d = sum(c.delta for c in active)/len(active)
   365→        zone = sum(1 for c in active if 0.4 <= c.delta <= 0.6)/len(active)
   366→        prob_open = sum(c.prob_mass for c in active)/len(active)
   367→        return {
   368→            "γ": round(g,4), "η": round(e,4), "Δ": round(d,4), "Σ": round(g+e,4),
   369→            "active": len(active),
   370→            "zone": round(zone,4),
   371→            "prob_open": round(prob_open,4),
   372→            "deformations": len(self.deformations),
   373→            "observations": len(self.observations),
   374→            "observers": list(self.observers.keys()),
   375→        }
   376→
   377→    def project(self, observer: str) -> Dict:
   378→        """Dependent first-person slice (‘show your work’)."""
   379→        if observer not in self.observers:
   380→            self.attend(observer)
   381→        return {
   382→            "format": "exoj-projection-v1",
   383→            "field": self.name,
   384→            "observer": observer,
   385→            "sense": self.sense(),
   386→            "recent_deformations": self.deformations[-12:],
   387→            "observations": self.observations[-5:],
   388→            "note": "Dependent linearisation of the parallel field. Wave-function remains open except at recorded observation events.",
   389→        }
   390→
   391→    def quilt_snapshot(self) -> List[Dict]:
   392→        return [c.to_dict() for c in self.cells.values() if c.touched > 0]
   393→
   394→    def save(self, path: str):
   395→        state = {
   396→            "name": self.name,
   397→            "seq": self.seq,
   398→            "sense": self.sense(),
   399→            "quilt": self.quilt_snapshot(),
   400→            "deformations": self.deformations,
   401→            "observations": self.observations,
   402→            "observers": self.observers,
   403→        }
   404→        Path(path).write_text(json.dumps(state, indent=2))
   405→        return path
   406→```

   491→## Lane doctrine (inherited, not optional)
   492→
   493→1. **Paired arms, identical worlds** — arms differ only in the mechanism under
   494→   test; the truth stream, confounders, and reward streams are shared.
   495→2. **Decision rules before the run** — the exact verdict rule is written into

   509→## Wave-69 — Bridge 3: the Unit Table GAN
   510→
   511→The adversarial pair that keeps the field honest, on the dynamic vector table
   512→(`gan/`): **Cell 01 (Generator)** is the moth — it nudges the lightest row and a
   513→hash-chosen subset of its neighbours toward a scaled-down copy of the densest
   514→row's direction, inside the amplitude budget, and rescales the touched rows'
   515→masses (conserving total mass: SPEC I1). **Cell 02 (Validator)** is
   516→specification-first: it re-derives proximity, conservation and dispersion and
   517→answers only `COMPILED | INDETERMINATE` with named codes — the generator's
   518→output never enters the pipeline when `compileRefused` is true.
   519→
   520→Constants are pre-registered, not knobs (provenance: quilt-murmur
   521→e40_summary.json): `MOTH_PROXIMITY = 0.798` (a2 = 0.798023), `MOTH_AMPLITUDE =
   522→0.49` (min = -0.493824 / rho = 0.495421), `SENSOR_LAG_MS = 3`,
   523→`DEADLOCK_STEPS = 12`. Verdicts fail closed: `E_CONSERVATION`, `E_BOUNDARY`,
   524→`E_HOMOGENISED`, `E_SXC_SPEC` (+ the sxc1 codes below). Deadlock or sensor lag
   525→fires the deterministic d20 (`gan/die.mjs` — same seed ⇒ same roll ⇒ same named
   526→drift, every roll receipted); a die-driven drift may cross the proximity
   527→boundary but is then scarred, not refused. Scars (`gan/scars.mjs`) are an
   528→append-only sha256 chain that survives any table rewind (I6: scars describe
   529→history, not state). State exchange uses `sxc1` envelopes (`gan/envelope.mjs`,
   530→I7): fail-closed on `E_SXC_FIELD / E_SXC_SEQ / E_SXC_PREV / E_SXC_HASH /
   531→E_SXC_SPEC`, float-free bodies for JS↔Python hash parity. Pathway: exoj emits →
   532→cocapn (Python mirror) verifies → quilt-dba stitches. Demo receipt:
   533→`node gan/demo.mjs` → `experiments/outputs/w69_bridge3_demo.json`.

   537→## The Atlas Kit — wave-66 (how to do it again without them)
   538→
   539→Wave-66 decomposed 29 SuperInstance works (plus the external research they
   540→leaned on) into **elementary parts as spreadsheet logic** — 528 parts across
   541→five canonical layers (substrate / mechanism / policy / interface / evidence),
   542→401 gates, 127 observed holes — and then let **jevs walk the layers**: soft
   543→deformations for concrete gates, explicit recorded collapse only at holes
   544→(the ExoJ law). Six lane agents + a keeper produced the corpus.
   545→

   547→the next instance needs zero agents to re-run the wave.

   550→node atlas.mjs verify        # re-verify the bundled artifact of record (6 checks)
   551→node atlas.mjs sweep         # re-run the pre-registered gate sweep (exoj policy=ledger)

   553→node atlas.mjs protocol      # the 7-move runbook: REGISTRY → DECOMPOSE → PREREGISTER
   554→                             #   → SWEEP → READ THE MAP → COMPILE → SEAL
   555→node experiments/e_x8_atlas_gatesweep.mjs   # E-X8: kit replay == artifact of record
   556→```
