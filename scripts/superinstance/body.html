WHY A NEW INSTINCT

Three primitives the classic grid never had

A formula computes. A listener notices. A weight in a visible cell can be argued with, nudged, and improved — by you, or by the sheet itself.

Rules are cells

Every rule — a trading clause, a poker read, a game's legality — is an inspectable cell you can click, question, and edit. Not a hidden function. The sheet is the spec.

Watching is a primitive

Listeners fire when a cell changes: flash a rule, halt a trade, refuse a payment, play a move. Reactivity isn't a plugin — it's the substrate. The demos below run on it.

Learning is visible

Weights live in cells you can watch drift. Improvement passes gates, and every promotion is a receipt in an fnv1a64 chain. You never have to trust the black box — the box is glass.
